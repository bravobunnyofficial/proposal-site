# 💍 Something Special

An interactive and emotional love proposal website that takes 
someone through a beautiful step-by-step romantic journey.

🌐 **Live Site** — [Visit Something Special](https://brilliant-snickerdoodle-465c3b.netlify.app/)

---

## 💌 About This Project

This is not just a website — it is a feeling.

Someone special opens the site and goes through a cinematic 
love story with music, animations, and emotional moments — 
ending with a heartfelt proposal.

---

## ✨ How It Works

1. A welcome screen appears with her name and a love message
2. Background music starts playing automatically
3. Emotional lines appear one by one with fade animations
4. A boy proposing animation appears with a ring box
5. She clicks **Yes ❤️** — flower rain, hearts, and a final love message
6. She clicks **No 💔** — heartbreak scene with ring drop sound

---

## 🎯 Features

- 🎵 Background music with smooth volume control
- 💍 Ring drop animation on rejection
- 🌸 Flower rain effect throughout the journey
- 💓 Heartbeat sound during proposal moment
- 📱 Fully mobile responsive
- 🎬 Step by step cinematic story flow
